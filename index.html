Privacy Policy for GTA 6 Hub

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0.0

1. Overview

GTA 6 Hub ("we", "our", or "the App") is an unofficial, community-driven fan companion application created to celebrate Grand Theft Auto VI. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our mobile application and how that information is handled.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, home address, or phone number) to access wallpapers, countdowns, guides, or news.

The App interacts with device features solely for functionality:

  • Device Storage & Photos (Write-Only): When you choose to download or save a 4K wallpaper, the App saves the image file to your device's Pictures or Wallpapers directory. The App does not access, read, or upload any of your personal photos or files.
  • Wallpaper Service: When you choose to apply a wallpaper, the App uses Android's native WallpaperManager API locally on your device to set your Home or Lock screen.
  • Local Cache: Preferences (such as favorited wallpapers, downloaded IDs, and viewing preferences) are stored locally on your device using encrypted SharedPreferences.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

To support server costs and community maintenance, GTA 6 Hub uses trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): AdMob may collect and process anonymous advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. For more information, please visit Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Supabase Cloud: We use Supabase to host public content such as game news, countdown timestamps, guides, and wallpaper catalogs. No personal user data is collected or stored on Supabase servers.

4. Children's Privacy

GTA 6 Hub does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app's themes and content are tailored for the general gaming community conforming to Google Play Families and Content Guidelines.

5. Security

All network communications between the App and remote content servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
